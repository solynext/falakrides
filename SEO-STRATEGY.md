# Falak Ride SEO plan

This plan separates pages by the visitor's task. Search language was reviewed qualitatively from current search-result snippets and the site's existing service, route, vehicle, and booking content. No keyword-volume tool or Search Console data was available, so these are intent-led targets, not volume or ranking claims.

| Page | Primary intent | Related language | Content purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| `/` | Find and book Umrah taxi/private transport | Umrah taxi Saudi Arabia; private transport; airport and intercity rides | Introduce the service and send visitors to the route, vehicle, service, and quote paths. |
| `/umrah-taxi-prices` | Compare how a private Umrah taxi is priced | Umrah taxi fares; SAR route fare; vehicle price; quote | Display only active published fares; explain quote inputs and mark unpriced options “Contact us for a quote.” |
| `/premium-umrah-transport` | Request a premium vehicle preference | Premium Umrah transport; GMC private transfer; family transfer | Explain the listed premium preference and what must be confirmed. |
| `/jeddah-airport-to-makkah-taxi` | Arrange an airport-to-hotel transfer | Jeddah Airport taxi to Makkah; JED pickup; Makkah hotel transfer | Give an arrival checklist and use matching fare records where published. |
| `/makkah-to-madinah-taxi` | Arrange a private intercity ride | Makkah Madinah taxi; hotel-to-hotel transfer | Explain exact pickup, itinerary, vehicle, timing, and quote confirmation. |
| `/madinah-to-jeddah-airport-transfer` | Reach Jeddah Airport from Madinah | Madinah to Jeddah Airport transfer; airport taxi | Cover flight and departure planning without promising a travel time or fare. |
| Existing `/locations/makkah` | Arrange a local private ride in Makkah | Makkah taxi; private taxi in Makkah | Keep the existing location URL and add route/pricing links rather than creating a duplicate page. |
| Existing `/vehicles` and `/packages/[slug]` | Compare a vehicle or vehicle package | Camry, Staria, GMC, Hiace; passenger and luggage fit | Use the current catalog and label capacity examples accurately. |
| `/travel-guides/[slug]` | Research a practical transport question | Airport booking, route pricing, vehicle choice, intercity taxi | Answer each question with a checklist and links to the relevant booking pages. |

## Content and accuracy rules

- Route fares come from active fare records. Do not infer a price for another vehicle, route, or trip type.
- Vehicle details and capacities come from the published catalog; ask the team to confirm the actual vehicle and luggage fit.
- Guides avoid guaranteed travel times, licensing claims, flight tracking, fixed fares, and other claims not established by the website.
- Each page uses one H1, descriptive section headings, contextual internal links, a canonical URL, and Open Graph metadata.
- `robots.txt` already allows public crawling and references the sitemap. The sitemap includes the new landing pages and guide URLs as well as published dynamic catalog pages.

## Qualitative search-result review

Current result examples include [UmrahTaxi.net](https://umrahtaxi.net/), which presents airport-to-city routes, and [Taxi Bhai's pricing page](https://www.taxibhai.com/pricing), which organizes fares by route. [All Abbas Transport](https://allabbastransport.com/) also presents Makkah–Madinah private transfer intent. These are qualitative examples of page/query language, not keyword-volume evidence or independent validation of competitors' claims. Recheck Search Console queries after deployment before expanding the target set.
